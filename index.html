Why use it?

What this actually is

A short list of long-term market picks. Built using past performance and simple rules. Free to follow. No ads. No sponsors.

Why it exists

Most market content is built for clicks. This isn't.

This list is built to answer one question: "What still makes sense to hold?"

How the picks are chosen

Instead of hype or predictions:

We look at how similar assets behaved in the past. We avoid short-term trades. We remove anything that no longer fits the rules. Nothing is added just because it's trending.

Is this legit?

Yes — but it's simple. This is not financial advice. There are no paid placements. Every pick can be traced back to public market data. If the data changes, the list changes.
